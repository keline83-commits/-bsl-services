A look at a recent deep clean we carried out for a client.

Open-Plan Kitchen & Living Deep Clean

A full deep clean of this open-plan kitchen and living space, covering the island worktop, cabinetry, appliances and hard flooring. Furniture and seating were carefully moved to reach every edge and skirting board, then finished with professional-grade, streak-free products safe for stone worktops and upholstery.

Riverside Apartment Deep Clean

A thorough deep clean of this riverside living room, from the upholstery and glass balcony doors to the floors and skirting boards. Sofas and the coffee table were carefully repositioned to clean underneath and behind, using high-quality products throughout to leave every surface spotless without damaging fabrics or finishes.
